Queue × Perspective: A Dimension Analysis

Why "Queue: X" is not a peer of "My Work" — and what the right model is

The Three Orthogonal Dimensions of Work Item Filtering

Every work item query decomposes into three independent axes. The current design conflates axes 1 and 2 by putting them in the same tab bar.

1. POPULATION SCOPE
"Which items are in the universe?"
• No queue — all items
• Queue X — items matching label pattern
Control: Queue pill bar (scope selector)
2. PERSPECTIVE
"What's my relationship to these items?"
• My Work — assigned to me + active
• Claimable — PENDING + my groups
• All Items — no assignee filter
Control: Tabs (within the scoped population)
3. AD-HOC FILTERS
"Further refinement within this view?"
• Status pills (constrained by scope)
• Priority pills (always interactive)
• Overdue / Breach toggles
Control: Filter bar + Summary badges

How Competitors Map These Dimensions

Tool Population Scope Perspective How They Compose
Jira SM Queues are JQL filters in a sidebar list "Assigned to Me" is just another queue They don't compose. You pick one queue. "My items in the Compliance queue" requires a custom JQL queue.
Zendesk Views are saved condition sets in sidebar "Your unsolved tickets" is a default view They don't compose. You pick a view. In-view filtering adds conditions but can't intersect two views.
Freshdesk Views with conditions "My Tickets" view via agent=me filter They don't compose. Each view is independent. You add ad-hoc filters within one view.
ServiceNow Service Channels → Queues "My Work" / "Team Work" list configs Partial. "My Work" and "Team Work" are separate workspace lists. Queue filtering within inbox is a known gap.
CaseHub (proposed) Queue pill bar (scope selector) Tabs: My Work / Claimable / All Full composition. Queue narrows population. Tabs filter perspective within it. Ad-hoc filters compose on top of both.

The Composition Model

Queue = outermost scope. Tabs = perspective within. Filters = refinement within both.

// The query pipeline:
items = allWorkItems
  .scope(queue?.labelPattern)   // Axis 1: population (queue pill or "all")
  .perspective(tab)            // Axis 2: my work / claimable / all
  .filter(status, priority, overdue) // Axis 3: ad-hoc refinement

What Changes

Before (current)
• Inbox tab: My Work | Claimable (no queue scope)
• Queues tab: giant cards → list → back button
• Two separate worlds, incompatible UX
• "My items in Compliance queue" = impossible
After (proposed)
• Queue pill bar (scope): Compliance Clinical ⚠ DevOps
• Tabs (perspective): My Work | Claimable | All
• Full composition: queue × perspective × filters
• "My items in Compliance queue" = select pill + My Work tab

The "All Items" Tab Question

The "All Items" tab is the triage perspective — it shows every item in the current population regardless of assignee. Without it, there's no way for a team lead to see the full picture of a queue. With a queue selected, "All Items" replaces the role that the old giant-card drill-down served.

When no queue is selected: "All Items" could either be hidden (agents don't need a firehose of every item in the system) or shown with a sensible default filter. The safest design: show it always, but it defaults to the user's groups — a "team overview" rather than a raw firehose.
